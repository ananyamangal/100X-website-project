## LAUNCH SEQUENCE RECOMMENDATION

1. **Start first**: Campaign 1A (OEM Authorization) + Campaign 4 (Tender Support) — lowest volume, highest intent, lowest CPC relative to value
2. **Add at week 2**: Campaign 1B (Dealer Program) + Campaign 2A (IS 14855) — broaden dealer funnel
3. **Add at month 2**: Campaign 2B, 2C, 2D (Municipal/NHM/Panchayat) — government buyer funnel
4. **Add at month 2**: Campaign 6 (Price Research) + Remarketing — top of funnel + recovery
5. **Supplement**: Campaign 5 (Make in India) + Campaign 1C (RA) — niche intent fills

**Prioritize call extensions on Campaign 4** — tender deadline searchers need immediate phone response.
