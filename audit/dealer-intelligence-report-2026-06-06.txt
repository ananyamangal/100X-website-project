  SECTION 7 — CRM PIPELINE DESIGN
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  Pipeline stages (linear, left to right):

  [IDENTIFIED] → [ENRICHED] → [CONTACTED] → [QUALIFIED] → [DEMO] → [PROPOSAL]
       → [NEGOTIATION] → [CLOSED-WON] → [ACTIVE ACCOUNT] → [CHURNED]

  Stage definitions:
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  IDENTIFIED   Record exists in gem_dealers. No outreach yet.
               Fields: canonical_name, l1_wins, departments[], opportunity_score
               Count today: 486 active dealers

  ENRICHED     Contact info obtained (phone or email). Ready to reach out.
               Fields: + phone/email/city/state/gst_number
               Sub-status: PARTIAL (1-2 contact fields) | FULL (3+ fields)
               Action: Queue for first contact

  CONTACTED    First outreach attempt made. No response yet, or responded.
               Fields: + contact_date, contact_channel, contact_rep
               Sub-status: NO_RESPONSE | CALL_BACK | INTERESTED | NOT_INTERESTED
               SLA: Follow up in 5 business days if no response (max 3 attempts)

  QUALIFIED    Discovery call completed. Confirmed buyer potential.
               Fields: + call_notes, annual_tenders, machine_source, order_size_est
               Disqualification reasons to log: paper_bidder | competitor_locked |
               volume_too_low | manufacturer_themselves

  DEMO         Demo scheduled or completed.
               Fields: + demo_date, demo_type (physical/video/loan), demo_notes
               Win signal: asks for pricing after demo

  PROPOSAL     Formal quote sent.
               Fields: + proposal_date, proposal_value, machines_quoted, terms_offered
               SLA: Follow up in 3 business days

  NEGOTIATION  Price/terms discussion active.
               Fields: + counter_offer, sticking_points, decision_timeline

  CLOSED-WON   Order placed and payment received (or confirmed).
               Fields: + order_date, order_value, machines_sold, payment_terms_agreed
               Action: Set is_100x_dealer = true

  ACTIVE ACCT  Repeat buyer. Monitor their GeM wins for upsell triggers.
               Fields: + lifetime_value, last_order_date, next_followup_date
               Automation: Alert when dealer wins a new GeM bid

  CHURNED      Was a customer, no longer buying.
               Fields: + churn_date, churn_reason
               Action: Re-engage quarterly during tender season
