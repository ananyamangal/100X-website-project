Description
Decorative Laminates are synthesised from paper and plastic resins, these are composite material
consisting of two or more layers bonded together to achieve improved strength, stability, and appearance.
These either consist of layers of paper or fabric impregnated with resin and covered by a protective overlay.
Decorative Laminates come in various colours, patterns, and textures, making them popular for surfacing
countertops, cabinets, furniture, and flooring due to their affordability and aesthetic appeal, they are
versatile and durable materials used in interior design and construction.  
Decorative Layer
This is the top layer, which features a printed design or pattern. It can mimic the appearance of various
materials, such as wood, stone or metal.  
Bonding Material of Decorative LayerAmino plastic Resin  
Core Layers
Below the decorative layer, there are several layers of kraft paper that are impregnated with phenolic resin.
These layers provide strength and stability to the laminate.  
Backer LayerThe bottom layer is also made of resin-impregnated kraft paper and serves as a protective backing.  
Bonding Material of Core and Backer
Layer
Both Phenolic and Amino plastic Resin
